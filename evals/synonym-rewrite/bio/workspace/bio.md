Rose Fisher is a seasoned data engineer with a deep passion for building reliable systems that help organizations make better decisions. She currently leads the platform team at Brightwater Analytics in Leeds, where she oversees the infrastructure that processes billions of events every day.

Over the course of her career, Rose has developed extensive expertise in stream processing, data quality and cost-efficient cloud architecture. Before joining Brightwater Analytics, she spent six years at Northfield Bank, where she designed the fraud detection pipeline that is still in use today.

Rose is a frequent speaker at industry events and has shared her insights at conferences across Europe. At Open Harbor 2025, she presented her widely acclaimed talk "Small Batches, Big Wins", which explored practical strategies for reducing pipeline costs without sacrificing quality.

Outside of work, Rose mentors early-career engineers through the Code Bridge programme and enjoys hiking in the Yorkshire Dales. She is passionate about making the data community more welcoming and inclusive for everyone.
