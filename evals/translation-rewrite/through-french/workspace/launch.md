# Introducing Pebble Notes 2.0

## A new chapter for your ideas

Today marks an exciting milestone for Pebble Notes. After 14 months of development and feedback from more than 3,200 beta testers, we are proud to introduce Pebble Notes 2.0, a complete reimagining of how you capture, organize and revisit your thoughts.

## What's new

Pebble Notes 2.0 introduces offline sync, which ensures that your notes are always available, even without an internet connection. Changes made on one device now appear on your other devices in under 2 seconds once you are back online. The new search engine indexes handwritten notes as well, allowing you to find anything you have ever written in an instant.

We have also redesigned the editor from the ground up. It now supports tables, checklists and code snippets, and it opens 40 % faster than the previous version on the same hardware.

## Pricing and availability

Pebble Notes 2.0 is available today on iOS, Android, macOS and Windows. The free plan continues to include up to 500 notes, while Pebble Plus costs €4.99 per month and removes all limits. Existing Plus subscribers receive the upgrade at no additional cost.

## Looking ahead

This release is only the beginning. Over the coming months, we will continue to listen to your feedback and refine every detail of the experience. Thank you for being part of the Pebble Notes community.
